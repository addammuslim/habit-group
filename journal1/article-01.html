08.08.2026 NEW OPENING

A NEW CHAPTER BEGINS IN KYOTO.

NOIRÉ opens its first property in Japan — a nineteen-room machiya in Higashiyama, reimagined for quiet, unhurried stays.

Eight years after VELA's first opening in Tokyo's Yanaka district, NOIRÉ is bringing the brand to Kyoto — a city that, in Adrian Wirawan's words, "has never once let us rush."

The new property occupies a converted machiya three streets back from Higashiyama's main thoroughfare, chosen specifically because it isn't on the main road. Nineteen rooms wrap around a single interior courtyard, each furnished individually by a different Kyoto craftsperson.

We didn't want to import a Jakarta idea of Japan. We wanted to sit still long enough to let Kyoto tell us what it needed.
— Sienna Kartawijaya, Chief Creative Officer

As with every VELA property, there is no front desk. Guests check in inside their room, and a single host is assigned for the length of their stay. The Kyoto house opens to guests on the first of October.
