ABOUT HABIT

WE BUILD BRANDS. WE SHAPE EXPERIENCES.

OUR STORY

HABIT began in 2012 as a single dining room in Jakarta, built on a simple conviction — that hospitality is a craft, not a transaction.

Twelve years later, that conviction has grown into a collection of brands spanning hospitality, dining, lifestyle and culture, across eight cities. What hasn't changed is the obsession with detail — the belief that a great experience is felt long before, and long after, it is seen.

01
OUR PHILOSOPHY

We design for feeling, not spectacle. Every space, menu and interaction is considered from the guest's point of view first — what they notice, what they remember, and what they tell their friends. Restraint is a discipline we take as seriously as ambition.

OUR APPROACH

Built to be lived in
