THE STORY

ULIN KA LEBAK believes a great stay has nothing to do with square footage.

Each ULIN KA LEBAK property holds no more than eighteen rooms, chosen for character over scale — a converted machiya in Kyoto, a townhouse in Tokyo's Yanaka district. Every room is furnished individually, with nothing standardised beyond the quality of the linen.
