HABIT COFFEE

THE STORY

HABIT COFFEE opened in 2015 with a single idea: one menu, one seating, one uninterrupted evening.

Set inside a restored colonial townhouse in Menteng, HABIT COFFEE serves a seasonal tasting menu built entirely around what's harvested that week from partner farms across Java. There is no à la carte — only a single, evolving story told over eight courses.

THE EXPERIENCE

Seating begins at 7pm sharp, with the room lit by candlelight alone. Guests move through eight courses over roughly three hours, each paired with a wine chosen by the house sommelier. Reservations open sixty days in advance and are limited to twenty-eight covers a night.
