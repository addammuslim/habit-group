MEET THE MINDS BEHIND HABIT.

Three leaders, one point of view — a short introduction to the people shaping the collection.

← BACK

HABIT was founded in 2012 by Adrian Wirawan, who still insists on personally tasting every new tasting menu before it's approved for AURA HOUSE. Twelve years and twenty-four brands later, he leads a team that has grown considerably, but the habit hasn't changed.

Sienna Kartawijaya joined as Chief Creative Officer in 2016, arriving from a decade in independent hospitality design. She has final say on every material, typeface and light fixture across the collection — a level of control she describes as "the only way restraint actually holds."

Consistency isn't a look. It's a set of questions you ask about every decision, no matter how small.

— Sienna Kartawijaya

Marcus Halim, Chief Operating Officer, joined most recently in 2020 and is responsible for the unglamorous work that makes the rest possible — logistics, staffing, and the quiet discipline of twenty-eight-cover reservations that somehow never run late.
